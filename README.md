# MARITIME RISK & COMPLIANCE INTELLIGENCE

**Screen · Correlate · Investigate · Assess**

Transforming vessel ownership, behavioural, operational and compliance information into structured maritime risk intelligence.

---

## Intelligence Question

### Does the available evidence indicate sanctions exposure or other maritime risk factors requiring further investigation?

Maritime risk and compliance intelligence is not based on a single vessel attribute.

A vessel's risk context may involve the relationship between:

- Vessel identity
- Ownership and management
- Sanctions status
- Flag history
- AIS behaviour
- Operational activity
- Ship-to-ship activity
- Corporate transparency
- Other independently documented indicators

The objective is to determine:

- What is directly documented
- Which entities or activities can be reliably attributed
- Whether a relevant sanctions connection exists
- Which indicators require further investigation
- Whether multiple independent indicators converge
- What level of risk assessment is justified by the available evidence

> **Core principle:** A risk indicator is an analytical signal — not proof of wrongdoing.

---

# 01 — Observation

The analysis begins with the available evidence.

The first question is:

> **What is directly documented or independently observed?**

Observable or documented information may include:

- Vessel identity
- IMO number
- Flag and flag history
- Ownership and management relationships
- Sanctions designations
- Corporate information
- AIS transmission patterns
- Vessel movement
- Documented ship-to-ship activity
- Port calls
- Satellite observations
- Other relevant maritime events

Each item is first recorded as an observation or documented record.

```text
VESSEL INFORMATION
        +
OWNERSHIP RECORDS
        +
AIS DATA
        +
OPERATIONAL CONTEXT
        +
COMPLIANCE INFORMATION
        ↓
AVAILABLE EVIDENCE
```

> **Analytical principle: Evidence is recorded before risk significance is assigned.**

---

# 02 — Identification

Risk assessment requires accurate attribution.

The analysis therefore establishes:

- Which vessel is being assessed
- Which entities are connected to the vessel
- Which role each entity performs
- Which identifiers support the attribution
- When the relationship was active

Primary identifiers may include:

- IMO number
- Vessel name history
- MMSI
- Call sign
- Corporate identifiers
- Jurisdiction
- Other reliable entity identifiers

```text
VESSEL
   ↓
IDENTIFIERS
   ↓
ENTITY RESOLUTION
   ↓
ROLE ATTRIBUTION
   ↓
TEMPORAL CONTEXT
   ↓
RELIABLE ATTRIBUTION
```

> **NOTE: A name match alone is not treated as sufficient evidence of a sanctions or risk connection. A risk assessment is only as reliable as the attribution supporting it.**

---

# 03 — Risk & Compliance Analysis

The identified vessel is assessed across multiple risk dimensions.

Sanctions Exposure

The analysis distinguishes between:

- Direct vessel designation
- Direct designation of an associated entity
- Current ownership or management connection
- Historical relationship with a designated entity
- Indirect corporate relationship
- Potential entity match requiring further resolution
- No identified relevant connection within the sources reviewed

A sanctions assessment considers both:

**Who is connected to the vessel?**

and:

**When was that relationship active?**

```text
VESSEL–ENTITY RELATIONSHIP
            +
RELEVANT TIME PERIOD
            +
SANCTIONS INFORMATION
            ↓
SANCTIONS EXPOSURE ASSESSMENT
```
> **Sanctions status should be reported as a source-based finding, not assigned by analytical opinion.**

---

## Dark-Fleet Indicators

Dark-fleet assessment is treated separately from sanctions designation.

The analysis may examine indicators including:

- Opaque or difficult-to-resolve ownership structures
- Repeated ownership changes
- Frequent flag changes
- Short-lived corporate relationships
- Changes in technical or operational management
- Limited corporate transparency
- AIS transmission anomalies
- Apparent AIS identity inconsistencies
- Documented false or disputed registration information
- Relevant ship-to-ship activity
- Satellite observations inconsistent with available AIS information

These indicators are assessed individually and in context.

> **No single indicator automatically establishes dark-fleet involvement.**

## Behavioural & Operational Context

Risk indicators are interpreted alongside the vessel's observed activity.

The analysis may correlate:

- AIS behaviour
- Movement patterns
- Ownership changes
- Flag changes
- Port activity
- Ship-to-ship activity
- Satellite observations
- Relevant sanctions or regulatory developments

```text
OWNERSHIP
     +
AIS BEHAVIOUR
     +
FLAG HISTORY
     +
OPERATIONAL ACTIVITY
     +
COMPLIANCE CONTEXT
        ↓
RISK PATTERN
```
The objective is to identify whether separate observations form a meaningful pattern.

---

# 04 — Correlation & Investigation

Individual risk indicators are not treated as conclusive.

The analysis investigates whether multiple sources support the same
interpretation.

| Evidence Area | Analytical Question |
|---|---|
| **Ownership** | Who controlled or managed the vessel during the relevant period? |
| **Sanctions** | Is there a documented relevant designation or relationship? |
| **AIS** | What does the movement and transmission history show? |
| **Flag** | What registration changes occurred, and when? |
| **Operations** | What activity is independently documented? |
| **Imagery** | Does available imagery support or challenge the operational picture? |

```text
OBSERVATION
     ↓
IDENTIFICATION
     ↓
CORRELATION
     ↓
INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
     ↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
```

The analysis distinguishes between:

**Confirmed evidence**
Directly supported by reliable information.

**Corroborated indicators**
Supported by more than one independent source.

**Analytical inference**
A conclusion suggested by the available evidence.

**Unresolved information**
Information requiring further investigation.

---

# 05 — Assessment

The final assessment considers whether the available evidence supports a meaningful compliance or maritime risk conclusion.

Possible assessment outcomes include:

- No significant risk indicator identified
- Limited indicators identified
- Relevant sanctions or compliance connection identified
- Multiple indicators requiring further investigation
- Elevated risk pattern supported by converging evidence

**The assessment communicates what the evidence supports — including what
remains unresolved.**

---

# 06 — Confidence

Confidence reflects the strength of the evidence supporting the final
risk assessment.

| Level | Intelligence Assessment |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | Multiple reliable and independent sources support the key findings, with consistent attribution and limited unresolved ambiguity. |
| **MEDIUM** | A relevant risk pattern is identifiable, but limitations remain in attribution, temporal coverage, source completeness or corroboration. |
| **LOW** | Available evidence is incomplete, conflicting or insufficient to support a reliable risk assessment. |

## Confidence Factors

Confidence is assessed using factors including:

- Reliability of source information
- Independence of sources
- Vessel identity certainty
- Entity-resolution confidence
- Accuracy of temporal attribution
- Ownership transparency
- Completeness of vessel history
- AIS data continuity
- Availability of satellite or other corroborating evidence
- Consistency between independent indicators
- Presence of unresolved contradictions
- Availability of sufficient operational context

**Confidence reflects the strength of the available evidence — not the severity of the potential risk.**

# Intelligence Assessment

Each investigation is structured as:

```text
VESSEL
   ↓
IDENTITY
   ↓
OWNERSHIP & MANAGEMENT
   ↓
SANCTIONS SCREENING
   ↓
AIS & BEHAVIOURAL CONTEXT
   ↓
OPERATIONAL CORRELATION
   ↓
RISK INDICATORS
   ↓
VALIDATION
   ↓
INTELLIGENCE ASSESSMENT
   ↓
CONFIDENCE
```

The final assessment distinguishes between:

**Observed or documented facts**
Information directly supported by available evidence.

**Sanctions status**
A source-based designation or verified regulatory finding.

**Risk indicators**
Observed characteristics or events requiring contextual assessment.

**Inference**
What the available evidence may reasonably suggest.

**Assessment**
The conclusion justified by the combined evidence.

**Confidence**
The strength of the evidence supporting that conclusion.

**Suspicion is not evidence. A risk assessment becomes stronger when vessel identity, ownership, behaviour and compliance information are independently attributed, temporally aligned and corroborated.**
